# Speech balloon

![Reference](reference.webp)

- The default dialogue treatment: a clean white oval balloon with a smooth dark outline and one pointed tail.
- Keep the working area pale filled lettering, or black lettering on white; never put dark text on a dark fill.
- One balloon per speaker. The tail points at the mouth of the character who speaks that line.
- The balloon sits in the panel's negative space and never covers a face, a hand or the acting gesture.
- Keep the balloon inside its own panel; never let it cross a gutter or a panel border.

Prompt fragment: Use clean white oval speech balloons with smooth dark outlines and one pointed tail each, small neat comic lettering, one balloon per speaker with the tail aimed at that speaker's mouth, sitting in the panel's negative space and never covering a face or crossing a panel border.
