# Burst shout

![Reference](reference.webp)

- A jagged spiked balloon for a shouted line, an accusation or sudden anger.
- Very heavy angular letters, dark outline, high contrast; the loudest mark in the panel.
- One burst balloon per panel at most. Two burst balloons in one panel cancel each other out.
- The spikes may reach into the panel's space but must never cut through a face or the acting hand.

Prompt fragment: Use one jagged spiked shout balloon with very heavy angular lettering and a thick dark outline, the loudest mark in the panel, with the spikes reaching outward but never cutting a face or hand; at most one shout balloon per panel.
