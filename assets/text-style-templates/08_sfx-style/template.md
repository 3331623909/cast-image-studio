# Sound effect

![Reference](reference.webp)

- Drawn sound effects that belong to the artwork rather than to a balloon: impacts, crashes, slams, a device hitting the floor.
- Two layers of outline so the letters survive against a busy background; hard flat fill.
- Record each sound effect as its own core text entry with delivery `sfx` and no speaker.
- Keep them clear of faces and of the panel's exact dialogue.

Prompt fragment: Draw the sound effect as part of the artwork, not in a balloon: heavy display letters with a double outline and hard flat fill so they stay readable against the background, kept clear of faces and of the dialogue lettering.
