# Thought cloud

![Reference](reference.webp)

- A scalloped cloud balloon with two or three small round connectors instead of a pointed tail.
- Soft pale fill, thin dark outline, quieter lettering than spoken dialogue.
- Never use the cloud shape for a spoken line, and never give a thought a pointed speech tail.
- The connectors lead toward the thinker's head.

Prompt fragment: Use a scalloped cloud thought balloon with two or three small round connectors leading to the thinker's head instead of a pointed tail, softer and quieter lettering than spoken dialogue; never give a thought a speech tail.
